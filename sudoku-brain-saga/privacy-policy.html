Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sudoku Brain Saga handles information when you use the game, including information handled through Google Play Games Services and Google Mobile Ads.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

In short: Sudoku Brain Saga is a puzzle game. Game preferences and certain game data may be stored locally on your device. If you connect Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services for gaming authentication and related features. The game also uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide advertising.

1. About this policy

Sudoku Brain Saga ("Sudoku Brain Saga", "we", "us", or "our") is a Sudoku puzzle game developed and published by Pradeept Bhanu.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is accessed, collected, used, stored, and shared by the App and by third-party services integrated into the App.

2. Google Play Games Services

Sudoku Brain Saga can connect to Google Play Games Services. If you choose to connect, Google Play Games may provide the App with information associated with your gaming profile, including your Play Games Player ID, display name, and profile information made available through the service.

We use Play Games information to identify your gaming profile, provide authentication, support achievements and leaderboards when enabled, and associate applicable game activity with your gaming profile.

Sudoku Brain Saga does not request or store your Google account password. Google controls authentication to Google Play Games Services, and Google's own policies apply to information processed by Google.

3. Local game data and preferences

The App stores certain information locally on your device so that it can remember settings and support normal gameplay.

This may include:

  • Game progress and puzzle-related state.
  • Sound Effects preference.
  • Haptic Feedback preference.
  • Rule-break error highlighting preference.
  • Timer display preference.
  • Other non-sensitive information required for game functionality.

Local application data can generally be removed by clearing the App's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android's normal application-data behavior.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Sudoku Brain Saga uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and other information needed for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and service operation, depending on applicable settings and region.

Advertising and consent behavior may differ depending on your location, device configuration, applicable law, and Google's advertising systems. Where required, the App may present Google's consent choices before requesting personalized advertising or other processing that requires user consent.

5. How information is used

Information handled by the App and its service providers may be used to provide and maintain the game, save game preferences and applicable progress, authenticate players, provide gaming features, display and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose technical problems, improve stability and functionality, and respond to support or privacy requests.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services necessary to provide App features.

Google Play Games Services

Used for gaming authentication and Play Games functionality.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Used to provide advertising and related measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising services.

These providers may process information according to their own policies, terms, and applicable laws.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Third-party Google services use their own security infrastructure and controls. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local game preferences and local game data remain on your device until they are removed by the App, cleared through Android application settings, or the App is uninstalled.

Information processed by Google Play Games Services or Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices.

The current version of Sudoku Brain Saga does not require users to create a separate account with us. If you have connected Google Play Games, Google Play Games account data is managed through Google's services.

If you believe that we directly hold information that should be deleted, you may contact us. We will review and handle the request in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Sudoku Brain Saga is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically designed to request personal information from children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Your choices

  • Choose whether to connect Sudoku Brain Saga to Google Play Games.
  • Change game preferences in the Settings screen.
  • Use available advertising consent controls where applicable.
  • Clear local application data through Android settings.
  • Uninstall the App.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, third-party SDKs, legal requirements, or our data practices change. When we make a material change, we will update the “Last updated” date shown at the top of this page.
