Game Concept

More than a Sudoku board

The product direction combines familiar Sudoku gameplay with lightweight progression and player motivation features.

🧩

Classic Sudoku

Clean, focused 9×9 puzzle gameplay with rule-break feedback and an optional timer.

🧠

Brain Focus

A game identity centered around concentration, logical thinking and consistent practice.

📈

Track Progress

Progression-oriented features help players understand their activity and improvement over time.

🏆

Daily Challenges

Daily gameplay creates a simple reason to return and build a regular Sudoku habit.
